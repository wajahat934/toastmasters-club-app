Our Club App

Rawalpindi Toastmasters Club  •  Book roles · Meeting agenda · Your Pathways progress
Everything you've already done in this club is waiting inside. Your past roles, your speeches and your Pathways progress are loaded — sign up once and it's all there. Scan the code above, or open wajahat934.github.io/toastmasters-club-app

Four steps — about two minutes

1

Open the link

Scan the QR code with your phone camera, or tap the link in our WhatsApp group.

2

Create your account

Tap Create an account. Enter your name exactly as it should appear on agendas, your email and a password. Birthday is optional — the club wishes you on the day 🎂

3

Wait for approval

An officer approves you (usually within minutes). Then everything unlocks.

4

Add it to your home screen

Android: menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
iPhone: Share ↑ → Add to Home Screen. It then opens like a normal app and keeps you signed in.

What you can do

Book a Role

  • See the next 3 meetings and every open slot
  • Tap Book to claim a role — no messaging anyone
  • Release it yourself if plans change
  • Vote for the winners during the meeting 🗳

My Profile

  • Your Pathways — level by level
  • Projects done, speeches given, roles completed
  • Set personal goals and tick them off
  • Send suggestions straight to the officers 💡
Have a role at the next meeting? If you're Toastmaster of the Day you set the meeting theme in the app; if you're Grammarian you set the Word of the Day — both appear automatically on the printed agenda.
Forgot your password? Message any officer — we reset it in under a minute.
Rather not do it alone? Grab an officer before or after any meeting and we'll set it up with you on the spot.
